Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026. This policy explains how PrimeStats handles information in connection with its Chrome extension and related services.

Contact: primestats.support@gmail.com

1. Overview

PrimeStats is a productivity analytics tool designed for supported TalkyTimes workflows. It records operational events and session metrics so authorized operators, supervisors, and organizations can understand productivity and performance.

2. Information PrimeStats may process

Depending on the enabled features and organizational configuration, PrimeStats may process operational information such as session start and end times, activity counters, supported interaction events, performance indicators, profile or operator identifiers, goals, and synchronization status.

PrimeStats is not designed to collect payment card information, government identification numbers, health information, or personal communications unrelated to the supported workflow.

3. How information is used

  • Provide session and productivity analytics.
  • Calculate supported metrics, goals, and performance indicators.
  • Synchronize authorized data across the organization.
  • Maintain reliability, diagnose errors, and provide support.
  • Protect the service from misuse or unauthorized access.

4. Browser permissions and website access

The extension uses limited permissions required for its core functionality. It is intended to run only on supported TalkyTimes pages and to communicate with configured PrimeStats cloud infrastructure. PrimeStats does not sell browsing history or use extension data for advertising.

5. Data storage and retention

Some information may be stored locally in the browser and, when synchronization is enabled, in the organization's configured cloud environment. Retention periods may depend on the organization's settings and the product version. Local session history may be limited to recent records to reduce unnecessary storage.

6. Data sharing

PrimeStats does not sell personal information. Information may be available to authorized members of the same organization, to infrastructure providers that process data on behalf of PrimeStats, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users and organizations should also protect account credentials and device access.

8. User and organizational choices

Organizations control who is authorized to use PrimeStats and how synchronized information is managed. Users may request support, correction, or deletion guidance by contacting the email listed above, subject to organizational and legal requirements.

9. Children

PrimeStats is a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as PrimeStats evolves. The effective date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to primestats.support@gmail.com.
