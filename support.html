Support

How can we help?

Use the information below to report a problem, request assistance, or ask about PrimeStats.

Contact support

Email us at:

primestats.support@gmail.com

Please include your extension version, a clear description of the issue, the steps that caused it, and screenshots when possible.

Before contacting us

  1. Confirm that Google Chrome is up to date.
  2. Confirm that PrimeStats is enabled in Chrome Extensions.
  3. Refresh the supported TalkyTimes page.
  4. Check whether the active session is still running.
  5. Note any visible error message.

Common questions

The panel does not appear

Refresh the supported page and verify that the extension is enabled and permitted on the site.

My session data looks incomplete

Confirm that the session was started before the activity occurred and that the page remained on a supported workflow.

Privacy or data request

For privacy questions, correction requests, or deletion guidance, contact the same support address and use the subject line Privacy Request.
